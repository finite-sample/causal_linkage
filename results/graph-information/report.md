Effect zero reports exact Fisher sharp-null size; positive effects report power.
Widths describe reconstructed estimates, not confidence intervals. The oracle
has zero linkage width while still facing randomization uncertainty.

Every method has the same accepted permutation (two errors), true identity map,
and eight units. Rate-only retains full donor conservation plus an at-most-two
identity-error budget. Near and far add different fixed pair candidate graphs;
both have two candidates per record and contain all true edges. These are
controlled information sets, not estimated or empirically calibrated graphs.

The small design is discrete: a larger effect need not raise power at every step.
The full summary includes 10% and 20% thresholds. The assignment-level file
preserves every result, including failures to reject. No assignment is dropped.
